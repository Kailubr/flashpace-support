Common questions

My scan came out messy.
Three things, once: lay the book flat, get the whole page inside the brackets, and let the light land on the paper rather than your shadow. The edit screen before every read lets you fix anything the scan got wrong.

Where did the table / caption / footnote go?
FlashPace deliberately keeps tables, figure captions, and footnotes out of the reading flow — they're for looking at, not flashing. The review screen tells you when it left something out, and you can paste anything back in.

What are the free plan's limits?
3 scans a day, the first 500 words of each scan, and speeds up to 500 words per minute — forever, no account needed. Pro removes every limit.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?
Open the Pro screen (Settings → FlashPace Pro) and tap restore purchases. Purchases follow your Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FlashPace. Cancelling takes two taps, no hard feelings.

Is my reading data private?
Your photos and their text never leave your phone. The whole story is in our privacy policy — it's short.
