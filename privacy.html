FlashPace · Privacy Policy

Your books never leave your phone.

Effective date: September 19, 2026

The short version

FlashPace photographs book pages and turns them into text entirely on your device. The photos you take and the text extracted from them are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared — with us or anyone else. We run no analytics, show no ads, and require no account.

What stays on your device

  • Photos of pages you scan, and the text extracted from them
  • Your reading statistics: pace, streaks, session history, calibration results
  • Your settings and preferences

This data lives only in the app's local storage and is included in your standard device backup (iCloud or local). Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Only what purchasing requires. If you subscribe to FlashPace Pro or buy a lifetime unlock, the purchase is processed by Apple, and the receipt is validated by RevenueCat, our payment infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase-transaction information — never your name, your email, your photos, or anything you read. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell
  • No accounts, passwords, or profiles required to use the app

Children

FlashPace does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is nothing in the app that collects it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Given what the app is, we don't expect it to change much.
